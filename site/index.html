How high should you bid in Setback?

Bid charts for nine sets of house rules and the result of every possible 6-card hand, from GPU simulations where every player looks ahead. Everything runs in your browser: the data are Parquet files queried by DuckDB-WASM. Source and method: setback-sim on GitHub.

Rules every variant shares

Each cell: how far to go in an auction with that trump holding, by the line that wins the most whole games: bid while the bid beats passing (expected points against passing ≥ 0); a higher bid never scores more, so bid only as high as it takes to win. A bid in (brackets): too few hands seen, taken from the weaker holdings next to it. Rows: trump honors held; columns: other small trumps. * = shooting the moon also pays. Blank = no such hand. Every distinct 6-card hand was played as bidder against hundreds of random deals. Type six cards like AS JS 2S KH 9D 4C (10 = 10 or T; suits S H D C or ♠ ♥ ♦ ♣).

Tables: presets, charts, chart_ev (one row per bid), condensed, and allhands (every hand by preset, 4- and 6-handed, rule-based and search players; a query reads only the parts it needs; where present it is a view joining hand_groups, each hand's trump and group, with group_stats, each group's numbers).
