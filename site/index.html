How high should you bid in Setback?

Bid charts for nine sets of house rules and the result of every possible 6-card hand, from GPU simulations where every player looks ahead. Everything runs in your browser: the data are Parquet files queried by DuckDB-WASM. Source and method: setback-sim on GitHub.

Each cell: the highest bid with expected points ≥ 0 for that trump holding, so how far to go in an auction (often the best opening is lower; click a cell for every bid's value). Rows: trump honors held; columns: other small trumps. * = shooting the moon also pays. Blank = fewer than 200 hands seen.

4-point Setback (High, Low to whoever captures it, Jack, Game; bids 2–4). Every distinct 6-card hand was played as bidder against hundreds of random deals. Type six cards like AS JS 2S KH 9D 4C (10 = 10 or T; suits S H D C or ♠ ♥ ♦ ♣).

Tables: presets, charts, chart_ev (one row per bid), condensed, and allhands (3.85 million rows: every hand, 4- and 6-handed, rule-based and search players; a query reads only the parts it needs). Ctrl+Enter runs.

Charts: 4 million deals per preset (2 million 6-handed), 16 sampled deals per decision. MIT licensed. Data: chart CSVs · every-hand tables · Parquet: charts, chart_ev, allhands.
